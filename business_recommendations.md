# Business Insights & Recommendations

> Run the Python/SQL analysis against the repository dataset before publishing numerical findings. This document intentionally avoids invented KPI values.

## 1. Protect profitable growth

Revenue growth should always be evaluated together with profit and margin. A category can be a sales leader while destroying value through discounts or high-cost products.

**Action:** monitor sales growth and margin together in the executive dashboard.

## 2. Review loss-making products

Products with negative aggregate profit deserve a pricing, discount, cost, or assortment review.

**Action:** create a monthly exception list for products with negative profit and meaningful sales.

## 3. Control excessive discounting

Discounts should be evaluated by resulting margin, not by sales volume alone.

**Action:** establish discount bands and identify the point at which incremental sales no longer compensate for lost margin.

## 4. Prioritize high-value customers

Customer ranking should consider both revenue and profit. A high-spend customer with weak profitability should not automatically receive the same treatment as a highly profitable customer.

**Action:** build targeted retention and cross-sell campaigns for high-value repeat customers.

## 5. Investigate regional differences

Regional performance can reveal differences in demand, product mix and profitability.

**Action:** compare regions using sales, profit, margin and category mix rather than sales alone.

## 6. Turn the dashboard into a decision tool

Every visual should answer a business question. The dashboard should make it easy for a manager to identify what changed, why it changed, and what needs action.
