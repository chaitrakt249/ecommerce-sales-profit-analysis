# E-Commerce Sales and Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profit performance using Python and Pandas.

The analysis focuses on identifying sales trends, profitable categories and sub-categories, and customer segment performance to generate actionable business insights.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains 9,994 records and 21 columns covering:

- Order and shipping information
- Customer details
- Customer segments
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Geographic information

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Plotly
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Exploration
- Inspected dataset structure and dimensions
- Checked data types
- Reviewed descriptive statistics
- Converted order and shipping dates into datetime format

### 2. Sales Analysis
- Monthly sales trends
- Sales by category
- Sales by sub-category

### 3. Profit Analysis
- Monthly profit trends
- Profit by category
- Profit by sub-category
- Profit margin analysis

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis
- Sales by customer segment
- Profit by customer segment
- Profit margin comparison across segments

## Key Business Insights

- Sales show clear monthly variation, with November recording the highest sales, followed by December and September.
- Technology is a strong contributor to overall sales performance.
- The Consumer segment contributes the highest overall sales among the customer segments.
- Profitability varies across categories and sub-categories, showing that high sales do not always result in equally high profits.
- Profit margins differ across customer segments, highlighting the importance of evaluating profitability alongside sales.
- The analysis demonstrates why businesses should evaluate both sales and profit when making data-driven decisions.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing sales periods and prepare inventory and marketing strategies accordingly.
- Identify high-sales but low-profit products and review their pricing and discount strategies.
- Prioritize profitable categories and sub-categories when planning product and marketing strategies.
- Monitor customer segment profitability rather than focusing only on sales volume.
- Use both sales and profit metrics to support better business decisions.
