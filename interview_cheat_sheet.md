# Interview Cheat Sheet

## Tell me about the project

"I built an end-to-end e-commerce analytics project covering data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL business analysis and Power BI reporting. I focused on sales, profitability, customer value, product performance, regional trends and the impact of discounts. The goal was to convert transaction-level data into recommendations a business team could act on."

## Why SQL?

"SQL allowed me to reproduce business KPIs directly from transactional data and answer business questions using aggregations, CTEs, CASE expressions and window functions."

## Why Python?

"I used Python and Pandas for data-quality checks, cleaning, feature engineering and exploratory analysis before building the reporting layer."

## Why Power BI?

"I used Power BI to turn the analysis into an interactive dashboard so business users could quickly monitor KPIs, trends and areas requiring attention."

## Why profit margin instead of only sales?

"Sales show demand, but profit and margin show whether that demand creates economic value."

## What is a window function?

"A window function performs calculations across related rows without collapsing them into a single grouped row. I used window functions for product ranking and month-over-month comparisons."

## What was the biggest business finding?

"One important finding was that higher sales do not always mean higher profitability. Some products and sub-categories generated losses, and discount levels also had an impact on margins. This shows why businesses should evaluate both revenue and profitability."

## What would you improve next?

"I would connect the dashboard to refreshed source data, add automated data-quality checks, introduce customer cohort and retention analysis, and monitor the KPIs on a recurring basis."

## What SQL concepts did you use?

"I used GROUP BY, HAVING, CASE statements, CTEs, aggregations, date functions, LAG and DENSE_RANK window functions."

## What did you learn from this project?

"I learned how to take raw transactional data, clean and analyze it, use SQL to answer business questions, and present the results through a Power BI dashboard with actionable recommendations."
